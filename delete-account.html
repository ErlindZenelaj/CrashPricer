Account deletion

Request deletion of your account

You can delete your CrashPricer account from inside the app, or ask us to delete it for you — including after you have uninstalled.

Fastest: delete it yourself in the app

Open CrashPricer and go to Settings → Delete account. The account is removed immediately and no email exchange is needed.

If you no longer have the app

Email us and we will verify the request before acting on it, because an account must never be deleted by someone who is not its owner.

  1. Email support@crashpricer.com with the subject “Account deletion”.
  2. Send it from the email address the account is registered to.
  3. We reply to that address to confirm the request came from you.
  4. Once confirmed, the account is deleted and we email you when it is done.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

The account itself, sessions and sign-in links, cases and photos stored on the server, marketplace requests and offers, in-app notifications, and analytics tied to the account. Accepted offers are removed together with the request they belong to. Cases and photos held only on your phone are removed with the app's data.

What is kept, and why

Limited moderation records — the type of action, its reason, timestamps and pseudonymous identifiers — may be kept after deletion where that is necessary to protect other users and prevent repeat abuse, and records we are required by law to retain. These do not identify you publicly. Deleted data also persists in encrypted backups until they expire on their normal cycle (TODO — backup retention window), after which it is gone from those too.

How long it takes

Deletion from inside the app is immediate. An emailed request is answered within 30 days of us verifying it, and usually much sooner.
