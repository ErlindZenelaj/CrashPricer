FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does SnapPrice estimate?

Visible bodywork damage. From your photos SnapPrice identifies the affected part, the damage type and the severity, then returns an estimated repair range in euros for that work.

Is the estimate a final price?

No. The estimate is a range with an evidence score attached — it tells you what the repair is likely to cost. The binding numbers are the offers repair shops send you afterwards.

What if the damage needs a physical inspection?

SnapPrice says so. Some cases can only be scoped for visible bodywork, and hidden damage behind a panel needs a shop to look at it. The app flags that instead of pretending the range is complete.

How do repair shops see my case?

Only when you publish it. Shops whose coverage area includes your location receive the case with the photos and the detected damage, and can respond with an offer.

Do I have to accept an offer?

Never. Offers sit in your list until you accept one, and you can withdraw a case at any point. Nothing is booked until you book it.

Where does SnapPrice work?

Across all 22 municipalities in Kosovo at launch, with estimates priced in euros. More regions follow.
